# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Students (young Muslim entrepreneurs under Abu Lahya's halal influencer-marketing mentorship), coaches, and owners. Daily use, often mobile, between outreach and study blocks. Students glance to see how the day is tracking and what's next; coaches spot at-risk students fast; owners take a platform-health snapshot.

## Product Purpose
A student performance & coaching platform for Abu Lahya's mentorship. It tracks daily outreach/work, a step-by-step roadmap, daily reflections, and surfaces at-risk signals to coaches. Success = a student opens it daily, logs work in seconds, and always knows the next action; a coach sees who needs help without digging.

## Brand Personality
Disciplined. Trustworthy. Understated. Evoke calm confidence, focus, and earned progress — NOT urgency, NOT gamified hype. The app sits between Ibadat and Dunya; it must feel respectful and grounded. Voice is direct and specific, never hype.

## Anti-references
Generic Tailwind SaaS templates; gamified productivity apps (streaks-confetti-amber-stars); bootcamp purple-gradient marketing sites; "AI coding assistant" landing pages. No gradients (incl. blue-to-blue), no gradient text, no pastel/magenta/teal/violet, no glassmorphism, no >1px side-stripe borders, no monumental 7xl/8xl hero metrics, no filled-circle "stitch" win motif.

## Design Principles
- **One focal point per view** — the hero metric is unmistakably the hero; no competing heroes.
- **Typography over ornament** — hierarchy from size/weight/tracking and tabular-nums, not effects.
- **Blue is signal** — `ima-primary` marks what matters (active nav, single hero metric, primary CTA, focus rings), not decoration.
- **Every border earns its place** — shadows only on hover, never at rest.
- **Respectful restraint** — understated over expressive; the tool serves the work, it doesn't perform.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG-minded: 44px touch targets on every interactive element; `aria-label`/`<label>` on inputs; ARIA on dynamic content (progressbar/timer/alert); `aria-hidden` on decorative icons; body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Reduced motion respected via `motion-safe:` on every `animate-*`. Reference feel: Linear (sidebar restraint), Stripe Dashboard (KPI hierarchy, tabular numbers), Vercel (spacing discipline).
